## 8. Findings / caveats / PROVISIONAL

- **PROVISIONAL (appearance adaptation):** the recessed plates were NOT re-run through mechanical/square_v1/cad/verify_v01.py (21 checks) or any FEA. The pocket removes plate material (square -1.45 mm to a 1.55 mm floor web, round -1.25 mm to a 1.75 mm web); stiffness, force-path, overload-stop and drop behaviour are unverified.
- **PROVISIONAL:** the print is assumed fused in place by the slicer (Kromacut multi-material 3MF). No slicer/Bambu test was run here; the 0.2 mm radial interference and 0.05 mm top step are nominal CAD values.
- **Source-model defect found in the round (LUMEN) plate:** `mechanical/lumen/build/parts/plate_v01.step` is a **2-shell solid** - it contains a closed internal conical void (apex z=16.100, ceiling z=16.700, base radius 48.0 mm, volume 1447.645895 mm3). The model intended a top dish (`Cone` default Z alignment cuts a void instead). Consequence for this task: the top surface is FLAT at z=17.000 (no conical dish), and the pocket cut opens that void as a thin annular slit in the pocket wall. The primary `round/plate_recessed.*` keeps the validated STEP intact; `round/plate_recessed_clean.*` is the same cut after filling the void (external surfaces unchanged) and is the recommended file for render/3MF if the slit is visible. Both are appearance artifacts only. This should be fixed upstream in mechanical/lumen (read-only here).
- **Task-fact discrepancy (round):** the task brief said the round plate has a `conical dish ~0.9 mm deep at centre`; measured geometry says the top is flat at z=17.000 everywhere inside d96 (probes r=0..45 all 17.000) and the dish-like geometry is the hidden internal void above. The pocket floor spec (15.75) is therefore still correct because it is rim-based, and pocket depth is uniform 1.25 mm - there is no rim-vs-centre depth difference.
- **Spec update (square):** floor 16.55 / depth 1.45 (elf print 1.40 mm, 7x0.2 mm), superseding the earlier 1.25 mm/16.75 draft. Volume delta matches pi*r^2*1.45 to <0.01 %.
- **Volume criterion:** square delta 8422.767 mm3 vs pi*43^2*1.45 = 8422.767 mm3 (+0.0000 %). Round delta 5844.959 mm3 vs pi*43^2*1.25 = 7261.006 mm3 (-19.50 %) - outside the +/-15 % band, because the pre-existing engraved ring (12.25 mm3) and internal void (1403.80 mm3) were already air inside the pocket footprint before the cut. Against the material actually above the floor the match is +0.0000 %; the void-filled clean variant is 12.2522 mm3 below nominal, exactly the pre-existing engraved ring volume.
- **Clearance results:** round: groove n/a mm (inside pocket footprint -> removed by cut), fillet 7.8000 mm, outer edge 9.0000 mm; square: groove 2.6000 mm (outside pocket footprint -> kept), fillet 5.8000 mm, outer edge 7.0000 mm. All outside-pocket clearances > 1 mm.
- **Internal-parts collision:** round: max internal part z 13.4000 mm (shell_v01), floor 15.7500 mm, margin 2.3500 mm; square: max internal part z 14.5000 mm (shell_v01), floor 16.5500 mm, margin 2.0500 mm. All internal parts are far below the pocket floors.
- **Round plate skirt/column:** skirt top z=14.000 and centre boss z=14.000; pocket floor 15.750 -> 1.75 mm clearance, i.e. the cut never touches the skirt or force column. Square: skirt/boss top z=15.000, floor 16.550 -> 1.55 mm.
- **Legacy round parts:** `guide_v01` / `ledflex_v01` exist in the LUMEN build (legacy light guide, user decision 2026-11-04 removed LEDs) and are marked `use_in_render: false` in parts.json; they were still checked for Z (max 13.4 mm).
- **Round source defect - what it is (a):** the validated STEP itself is a 2-shell solid: a closed conical void inside the plate (apex z=16.1000, ceiling z=16.7000, base radius 48.0000 mm, volume 1,447.6459 mm3). It is present in the source file BEFORE any cut and was not introduced by this task. The pocket floor at z=15.75 is solid material, 0.3500 mm below the void's lowest point (centre apex 16.10).
- **Round source defect - pocket wall slit (b):** the cut opens that void at the pocket wall as an annular slit from z=16.6375 to z=16.7000 (height 0.0625 mm) at r=43, extending outward under a 0.30 mm thick top skin. That is sub-nozzle scale (~0.06 mm) and is covered by the 0.2 mm print radial interference where the print meets the wall, but it is an undercut that cannot be machined as-is and may show as a hairline in extreme macro renders -> PROVISIONAL.
- **Round source defect - clean variant (c):** external geometry and bounds are identical between the faithful primary and the void-filled clean variant (bbox identical: yes; top surface probes r=0/20/40 both 17.000). The clean variant only fills the closed void (external surfaces untouched), removes the wall slit (yes) and is offered as an alternative for render/3MF if the hairline matters. Canonical shell.json keys describe the FAITHFUL primary.
- **Only intentional overlap:** the 0.2 mm radial interference (print r=43.2 vs pocket r=43.0) is the sole designed interpenetration; axially the print sits on the solid pocket floor, which is the exact Z reference for placement (print local z 0..thickness -> world floor_z..floor_z+thickness).
- **STL bbox in Blender vs exact BRep bbox:** the round plate/blank STLs are tessellated with inscribed chords (0.02 mm linear tolerance), so Blender may report e.g. x_min=-51.935 instead of -52.000 on the round plate; the exact BRep bbox is +/-52.000 (STEP re-import confirms), and the square plate reads exact +/-50.000 because its sides are flat. This is mesh approximation, not geometry.
- **Depth is exactly 1.25 / 1.45 at all radii:** because the round top is flat at z=17.000 (no dish), depth samples at r=0/20/40 are identical; the pocket floor plane is flat to 0.000000 mm (PLANE face) and prints need no Z tilt.
